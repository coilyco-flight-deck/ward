# Aguard ops forgejo

`aguard ops forgejo` is the AOS-container Forgejo operator surface.

- It is generated by specgen in the AOS builder and shipped as `aguard`.
- Its runtime help and execution do not invoke or mention Ward.
- Ward's native `agent` control plane keeps typed issue, reservation, PR, and
  landing adapters for its own workflow.

Forgejo being Ward's canonical tracker does not make Ward the generic Forgejo
operator CLI.
